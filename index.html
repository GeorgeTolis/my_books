Welcome to My Library

Stories Written from the Heart

"This website is meant to store all the books of the author George Tolis (that's me 0_0)."

If you stumble upon this website by chance, first of all welcome to my world. Second, I hope you give my writing a chance.

I have the goal of writing one new story every year, finishing it in the summer, or maybe early autumn of the corresponding year. My stories are not connected in any way between one another and their purpose is for me to express my feelings that I keep hidden from the outside world in an indirect way.

If at least one of my stories moves you, then my goal is accomplished.

GT

George Tolis

Author & Storyteller
Annual Goal: 1 Story / Year

Warning for non Greek speakers

I am Greek, so my books' original languages are also Greek, because I think it is the language that I can express myself best in.

As of writing this, I assure you there are plans to translate them to English, but I am focussing on my studies and work right now, so it is difficult for me to find time to do this. Nevertheless, when English versions drop, I will make appropriate changes to this website.
